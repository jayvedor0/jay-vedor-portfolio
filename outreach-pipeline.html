Follow-up cadence

Day 0: call; if no answer, send an email. Day 2: second call and a text. Day 5: final call and a closing email.

No reply after the fourth attempt closes the lead, so the queue only holds people worth calling.

  1. Log every touch

    Source, status, attempts, and last touch sit on one row, so anyone can pick up a lead mid-conversation.

  2. Work the due list first

    Leads with a follow-up due today are the default view: the calls that make or lose a booking.

  3. Close the loop

    Booked leads get a confirmation and reminder; leads with no response are closed instead of chased forever.
