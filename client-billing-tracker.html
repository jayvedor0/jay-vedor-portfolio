How each amount is calculated

Amount needed = MAX(Required trust − Trust balance + Outstanding + WIP, 0)

Open billing is outstanding balance plus unbilled work in progress. The same formula runs in the spreadsheet.

  1. See the signal

    Balances, unbilled work, outstanding amounts, and status sit together instead of across separate notes and inboxes.

  2. Calculate the gap

    Each account’s amount needed is calculated against its required trust target, including open billing.

  3. Turn it into action

    Status and next-action fields turn the sheet into a follow-up queue rather than a passive record.
